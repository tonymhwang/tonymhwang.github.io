<div class="lang-panel" lang="en" markdown="1">

Hello, my name is Ming-Hung. I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Chung Cheng University (CCU), where I also serve as Head of Research and Development in the Office of Information Technology and Director of the Big Data Technology Research Center. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University in 2017, and my M.S. in Communication Engineering (2010) and B.S. in Computer Science (2008) from National Tsing Hua University.

I lead the **Digital Society and Security Lab (DIGI-SSL)**, which conducts research at the intersection of cybersecurity and computational social science. We combine large language models, agentic AI, graph theory, and data science to study large-scale social media platforms and digital infrastructures, both empirically and theoretically. Our topics include **information dissemination security**, **content safety**, and **platform integrity**. Through multi-platform data analysis, we aim to build a safer, fairer, and more sustainable digital society.

Our research has been published in international journals and conferences and covered by NHK, Nikkei Asia, Voice of America, CommonWealth Magazine, Business Today, and Apple Daily. Our work has also received awards and grants, including the IEEE Tainan Section Best Young Professional Member Award, the CCU Young Scholar Award, the CCU Excellent Teaching Performance Award, an Honorable Mention in the Taiwan Comprehensive University System Young Scholars' Innovative R&D Results Selection, and the NSTC Excellent Young Scholars Program.

<div class="lang-panel" lang="zh-Hant" markdown="1">

嗨，我是銘宏，現任國立中正大學資訊工程學系副教授，兼任資訊處技術發展組組長與巨量資料科技研究中心主任。我於 2017 年取得國立臺灣大學電機工程博士學位，並於 2008 年及 2010 年在國立清華大學取得資訊工程學士與通訊工程碩士學位。

我主持**數位社會與安全實驗室（DIGI-SSL）**，從事資訊安全與計算社會科學的跨領域研究。實驗室結合大型語言模型、代理式人工智慧、圖論與資料科學等方法，對大型社群媒體平台與數位基礎架構進行實證與理論研究，議題涵蓋**資訊傳播安全、資訊內容安全與資訊平台安全**三個面向。透過跨平台的數據分析，我們致力於建構更安全、公正且永續的數位社會。

研究成果發表於國際期刊與會議，並獲 NHK、日經亞洲、美國之音、天下雜誌、今周刊、蘋果日報等媒體報導。相關研究與教學成果曾獲 IEEE Tainan Section Best Young Professional Member Award、國立中正大學青年學者獎與教師優良教學獎、臺灣綜合大學系統年輕學者創新研發成果選拔佳作獎，以及國科會優秀年輕學者研究計畫補助。

</div>
