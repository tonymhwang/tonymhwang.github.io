About
------

Hello, my name is Ming-Hung. I am an Associate Professor at the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering and the Head of Research and Development at the Office of Information Technology at National Chung Cheng University. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University in 2017, after completing my M.S. degree in Communication Engineering in 2010 and my B.S. degree in Computer Science in 2008, both from National Tsing Hua University.

I lead the **Digital Society and Security Lab (DIGI-SSL)**, which focuses on interdisciplinary research at the intersection of cybersecurity and computational social science. Our lab integrates methodologies such as large language models, agentic AI, graph theory, and data science to conduct both empirical and theoretical studies on large-scale social media platforms and digital infrastructures.

We examine a wide range of topics, including **information dissemination security**, **content safety**, and **platform integrity**. Through cross-disciplinary approaches and multi-platform data analysis, our mission is to build a safer, more equitable, and sustainable digital society.

Our research has been published in international journals and conferences and featured in numerous media outlets, including NHK, Nikkei Asia, Voice of America, CommonWealth Magazine, Business Today, and Apple Daily. These achievements have also been recognized with awards including IEEE Tainan Section Best Young Professional Member Award, Young Scholar Award of National Chung Cheng University, Excellent Teaching Performance Award of National Chung Cheng University, Masterpiece Award of Taiwan Comprehensive University System Young Scholars' Innovative R&D Results Selection, and Excellent Young Scholars Program of NSTC.

We are currently recruiting graduate and undergraduate students interested in cybersecurity and computational social science. Feel free to contact me via tonymhwang at gmail.com.

關於我
------

嗨，我是銘宏，目前於國立中正大學資訊工程學系擔任副教授，兼任資訊處技術發展組組長與巨量資料科技研究中心主任。我於 2017 年取得國立台灣大學電機工程博士學位；在進入台大前，我分別於 2008 年及 2010 年在國立清華大學取得資訊工程學士與通訊工程碩士學位。

我主持**數位社會與安全實驗室（DIGI-SSL）**，專注於資訊安全與計算社會科學領域的結合，進行跨領域的創新研究。本實驗室整合大型語言模型、代理式人工智慧、圖論，與資料科學等方法，針對大型社群媒體平台與數位基礎架構進行實證與理論研究。我們關注的議題涵蓋以下幾個主要面向：**資訊傳播安全、資訊內容安全，與資訊平台安全**。透過跨領域的方法與跨平台的數據分析，我們致力於建構一個更安全、公正與永續的數位社會。

我們的研究成果發表於國際期刊與國際會議，並獲得多家媒體報導，包括 NHK、日經亞洲、美國之音、天下雜誌、今周刊和蘋果日報等。相關研究與教學成果也曾獲 IEEE Tainan Section Best Young Professional Member Award、國立中正大學青年學者獎、國立中正大學教師優良教學獎、臺灣綜合大學系統年輕學者創新研發成果選拔佳作獎、國科會優秀年輕學者研究計畫等獎項與補助。

我們目前正在招募對資訊安全與計算社會科學感興趣的博士生、碩士生與專題生，歡迎透過 tonymhwang at gmail.com 與我聯繫。
