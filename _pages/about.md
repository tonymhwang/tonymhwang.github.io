<div class="lang-panel" lang="en" markdown="1">

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Chung Cheng University (CCU), where I lead the **Digital Society and Security Lab (DIGI-SSL)**. My research lies at the intersection of **information security**, **computational social science**, and **AI safety**: we study how information, people, and algorithms interact on large-scale online platforms, and how to make that ecosystem harder to manipulate.

Our work follows three directions, each with a growing focus on the risks introduced by generative models:

- **Information dissemination security**: detecting coordinated manipulation such as sockpuppets, cyber armies, and political propaganda, and analyzing the evaluative framing of LLM-generated political commentary.
- **Content safety**: identifying harmful and misleading multimodal content such as harmful memes and out-of-context image–text pairs, and testing how robust vision-language-model-based detectors are against adversarial manipulation.
- **Platform integrity**: tracing the provenance of LLM-generated text and building reliable rankings from noisy crowdsourced judgments.

We combine large language models and LLM-based agents, graph analysis, and data science in both empirical and theoretical studies across multiple platforms.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University in 2017, and my M.S. in Communication Engineering (2010) and B.S. in Computer Science (2008) from National Tsing Hua University.

Our research has been published in international journals and conferences and covered by NHK, Nikkei Asia, Voice of America, and CommonWealth Magazine. Recognition includes the IEEE Tainan Section Best Young Professional Member Award and the NSTC Excellent Young Scholars Program.

<div class="lang-panel" lang="zh-Hant" markdown="1">

我是國立中正大學資訊工程學系副教授，主持**數位社會與安全實驗室（DIGI-SSL）**。研究橫跨**資訊安全**、**計算社會科學**與 **AI 安全**，關注資訊、人與演算法在大型網路平台上如何互動，以及如何讓這個生態系更難被操弄。

我們從三個面向切入，並特別關注生成式模型帶來的新風險：

- **資訊傳播安全**：偵測分身帳號、網軍與政治宣傳等協同操弄行為，並分析大型語言模型生成之政治評論的評價框架。
- **資訊內容安全**：辨識有害迷因、脫離脈絡圖文等有害與誤導性的多模態內容，並檢驗以視覺語言模型為基礎的偵測系統面對對抗攻擊的韌性。
- **資訊平台安全**：追蹤大型語言模型生成文字的來源，並從雜訊多的群眾外包判斷中建立可靠的排序與評估。

我們結合大型語言模型與代理人系統、圖論與資料科學，進行跨平台的實證與理論研究。

我於 2017 年取得國立臺灣大學電機工程博士學位，並於 2010 年及 2008 年在國立清華大學取得通訊工程碩士與資訊工程學士學位。

研究成果發表於國際期刊與會議，並獲 NHK、日經亞洲、美國之音、天下雜誌等媒體報導；曾獲 IEEE Tainan Section Best Young Professional Member Award 與國科會優秀年輕學者研究計畫等肯定。
